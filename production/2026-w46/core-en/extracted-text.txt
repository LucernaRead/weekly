On Friday, November 13, a ghost
comes to the cinema — early. «
Ebenezer » is a dark new telling of
Charles Dickens's « A Christmas

Ebenezer Scrooge, the miser who
hates Christmas. Christmas is still
six weeks away. The film does not
wait.
Dickens wrote the story in 1843, in
London. Scrooge is visited in one
night by three ghosts : the Ghost of
Christmas Past, the Ghost of
Christmas Present, and the Ghost
of Christmas Yet to Come. By
morning, the cold old man is warm
again. The book made Christmas
what it is in Britain — and the films
keep remaking it.
The second film of the week is very
different. « How to Rob a Bank » is
a heist comedy : bank robbers who
film their crimes and post them
online. The police follow the likes.
It is fast, funny and very 2026 —
crime as content.
Which film would you choose ? A

again — half for the ghost, half for

WHY MUNICH ? · 为什么去慕尼黑？
The NFL plays games outside the
United States every year, so new fans
can see the sport live. Munich hosted
NFL games in 2022 and 2024, and will
host again in 2028. This year there are
nine international games in all — a
record.

Three cities, three sports, one
Sunday — and American football in
the home of Bayern Munich.
In Munich, the NFL plays at the
Allianz Arena : the Detroit Lions,
the home team, against the New
England Patriots. The game starts
at 3:30 p.m. local time — 9:30 a.m.
in New York. It is the Lions' first
game in Germany, and a special
game for their German-American
star, Amon-Ra St. Brown, whose
mother is German. The game is
part of NFL Week 10, and it is the
week's most talked-about fixture.
Back in the United States, Week 10
continues the same day : the Chiefs
play the Falcons in Atlanta, the
Vikings play the Packers at
Lambeau Field, and the 49ers play
the Cowboys in Arlington. The
week began on Thursday,
November 12, with the
Commanders at the Giants, and
ends on Monday, November 16,
with the Chargers at the Ravens.
In Turin, Italy, tennis begins its
season finale : the ATP Finals, from
Sunday, November 15, to Sunday,
November 22. The eight best
singles players of the year play in
two groups of four, and the final is
on November 22. The field was
settled on Monday, November 9,
after the Paris Masters.
And in Madrid, football takes the
stage : Spain play England in the
Nations League on Sunday night,
three days after England play
Croatia at Wembley on Thursday,
November 12. Scotland play North
Macedonia at Hampden Park on
Friday, November 13. One Sunday
is not enough — good thing the
week has seven days.

Friday, November 13 :
Pudsey, 26 and
Kindness
By The Lucerna newsroom · Reading level : B1

On Friday, November 13, Britain
turns yellow. It is the BBC Children
in Need appeal show, live from
Salford, on BBC One and iPlayer —
a night of music, comedy and
stories, to raise money for children
and young people across the UK.
The star is a yellow bear with a
spotted bandage over one eye :
Pudsey Bear.
This year's idea is simple :
Challenge Yourself 2026. Take on a
challenge inspired by the number
26 — 26 miles, 26 cakes, 26 songs.
Run with Pudsey and Joe Wicks
asks people to walk, run or roll two
miles a day for two weeks, until
they reach a marathon. Every mile,
every pound, helps a child who
needs someone by their side.
The presenters are Alex Jones, Sara
Cox, Rylan and Mel Giedroyc, with
Tyler West. Schools join in too : in
the week of November 9, children
run, bake and dress up, and the
money goes to projects near their
homes. One night, one bear,
thousands of small challenges.
Friday, November 13, is also World
Kindness Day. Kindness is the
week's other word : a small act, a
kind message, help for a stranger.
On social media, young people post
their 26 challenges with the same
sentence : « Challenge yourself —
and be kind. » That is the most
repeated sentence of the week.
And the children ? They watch
Pudsey, and they ask the best
questions : « Why does Pudsey
wear a bandage ? » « Can we do 26
too ? » « How much money will we
raise ? » The answer to the last
question is : we will know on
Friday night. The total will still be
there in the morning — and so will
the kindness.
