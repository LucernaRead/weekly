A2 · 展會的話 · Fair words
This week's fairs give you four useful
words: 旅展 (travel fair), 攤位 (booth),
集章 (stamp-collecting) and 吉祥物大遊行
(mascot parade). Learn them once, and
the whole travel fair makes sense.
Four fair words: 旅展， 攤位， 集章，
吉祥物大遊行.
旅展 is short for 旅行展覽會 (travel
exhibition). A 攤位 is one stall inside the
fair; the Taipei fair has 1,600 of them.
集章 means collecting stamps at set
points — collect all six Greenway stamps
and you win a prize.

B1 · 球賽與節氣 · Games & seasons
Sport and seasons share this week's
headlines. Group the words and they
stick: winning （封王、完封） and winter
（立冬、進補）.
Group the words: winning （封王/完封）,
winter （立冬/進補）.
封王 means clinching the championship;
完封 means shutting the other side out, 0
runs. 立冬 is the solar term that starts
winter; 進補 is eating nourishing hot food
before the cold months.

B2 · 港台說法 · Local talk
Taiwan and Hong Kong each have their
own way of saying things. This week:
打卡 (check in with a photo), 討糖
(trick-or-treat), 踩街 (street parade in
costume) and 泡湯 (soak in a hot spring).
Four local words from this issue: 打卡，
討糖， 踩街， 泡湯.
打卡 started as clocking in at work, but at
a fair or festival it means posting a photo
to say 'I was here'. 討糖 is what children
say at shops at Halloween; 踩街 is the
costumed parade itself; 泡湯 is the
autumn pleasure of a hot-spring bath.

易錯診所 · Error clinic
「龍隊完勝兄弟6比0」
→「龍隊完封兄弟6比0」
完勝 means winning big; 完封 means the
other side scored zero.
「立冬就是冬至」
→「立冬是冬天開始，冬至是白天最短的一
天」
Lidong starts winter; the Solstice is the
shortest day. Different terms.
「金融科技週在台北開幕」
→「金融科技週在香港開幕」
Check the city: FinTech Week opens in
Hong Kong, not Taipei.
本週句式 · Sentence frame
「先去集章，再去看表演。」 — the
frame 先…再… gives the order of two
actions: first X, then Y. Make your
own: 先比價，再結帳 (compare prices
first, then pay).
