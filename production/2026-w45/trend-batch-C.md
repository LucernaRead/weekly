## 2. 日本語 (Japan ecosystem)

### Headline candidate
**「文化の日と紅葉の週末：ゆるく整える日本の11月」**
(Culture Day mid-week + peak autumn leaves + the winter-food trend — a
"slow down and reset" week, matching Japan's own current lifestyle buzz.)

### Supporting trend items
1. **冬の食トレンド「ゆる整え食」** — Ajinomoto's winter 2026 food-trend
   announcement (21 Oct 2026, Tokyo): consumers shifting to "no-strain"
   wellbeing eating — steam baskets, mala-tang, one-pot soups; pot dishes on
   tables 1.5× vs last year Aug–Oct, supermarkets rolling out nabe a month
   early; "サクサクdeコパン" past 13 million servings in ~1 year.
   Source: 食品新聞 via Infoseek News, 26 Oct 2026.
   Angle: food vocabulary + the "ゆる" (loose/easy) cultural keyword.
2. **秋のライフスタイル：モード回帰と「考えなくていい」正解消費。**
   Late-October X buzz: peplum knits + check/tweed jackets for 30s–40s;
   "correct outfit" no-think consumption under price rises; one-dish meals.
   Source: note.com weekly lifestyle trend, 22 Oct 2026.
   Angle: fashion + daily-life Japanese; keep claims attributed to the post,
   not presented as survey data.
3. **11月は全国的に高温見込み（気象庁3か月予報）。**
   JMA 3-month forecast (20 Oct 2026, via 時事通信): weak seasonal winds,
   above-average temperatures nationwide Nov–Jan; more rain on Pacific side.
   Angle: weather small talk + "暖かい11月" vs the usual cold-season script.
4. **紅葉が見頃の週末。** Naruko Gorge (Miyagi) forecast: peak across the
   Oct 31 – Nov 8 weekends including Culture Day (source: 気象予報士 blog,
   18 Oct 2026, based on observations to 17 Oct). Kobe Nunobiki Herb Garden
   autumn-light-up starts Sat Nov 7 (source: 夜景FAN, 18 Oct 2026).
   Angle: 紅葉狩り planning language, regional (Tohoku + Kansai) spread.
5. **九州の旅が急伸、でも予算は抑制。** Domestic-travel searches: Kyushu
   demand nearly doubling year-on-year; average trip budget down; "nearby
   satisfaction" + autumn leaves + onsen + gourmet in one trip.
   Source: note.com weekly lifestyle trend, 22 Oct 2026.

### Calendar nodes inside Nov 2–8
- Tue Nov 3: 文化の日 (Culture Day, national holiday) — e.g. family orchestra
  event in Suginami, Tokyo (PR TIMES, 26 Oct 2026); Kanazawa: 市民美術の日
