  1. P1 numbers-box title size 23→20 (tsize=20) — the W43 PT×EN build made
     the identical change to the same box; at 23pt the English title ink
     reached x=997 past the box border at 961.
  2. P7 quote-row spacing 128→104 — the English readers'-corner stack ends
     ~87px lower than the core's, so 128 spacing drove the last quote

     ~19px above the preview box top.

     than the Chinese ones and the key flow overflowed past its 2552 bottom
     (3 FLOW OVERFLOW warnings); at 13/1.12 the flow ends clean with the
     lowest key baseline at 2546.6, ~30px clear of the footer rule.
  5. Apostrophes are ASCII (') throughout the English apparatus, not
     U+2019 — the CJK font renders U+2019 with a visible gap ("week' s"),
     while W43 PT×EN uses ASCII apostrophes throughout.
