# W43 core editions → W45 promises register

Purpose: every next-issue preview (下期预告) and readers'-corner (读者园地) promise
printed in the W43 core editions, extracted 2026-10-26 for the W45 production cycle
(issue week Mon Nov 2 – Sun Nov 8, 2026), so W45 can honor them per SOP §10.

Method: each core's WEEKLY-KIT.md read in full, plus the P7 SVG text of the shipped
render (preview box at the foot of P7, y≈2440–2520) quoted verbatim below. Weekday
checks by `date -d` (2026 calendar): Oct 26 Mon, Oct 27 Tue, Oct 28 Wed, Oct 29 Thu,
Oct 31 Sat, Nov 1 Sun, Nov 2 Mon, Nov 3 Tue, Nov 4 Wed, Nov 7 Sat, Nov 8 Sun,
Nov 9 Mon, Nov 11 Wed, Nov 15 Sun.

Classification key (relative to the W45 issue week Nov 2–8, 2026):
- **IN W45** = dated Nov 2–8, 2026 → W45 lead / live coverage.
- **W44 JUST PASSED** = dated Oct 26 – Nov 1, 2026 (the skipped W44 week) → cover as
  just-passed follow-up/report, not as a preview.
- **EARLIER** = before Oct 26 → already past at W43 press time; report only if the
  preview promised a result.
- **BEYOND W45** = after Nov 8 → not yet due in W45; do not fabricate a fulfillment,
  carry as a forward note.

Important sequencing note: every W43 preview below was written for the *next* issue
(W44, Oct 26 – Nov 1), which was skipped. W45 therefore inherits them one step late.
Nothing below invents a fulfillment: where a promise names a W44 event, the honest
W45 treatment is a just-passed report, and where a promise names a date beyond W45
(PT Nov 15, ZHCN Nov 11) the W45 action is to note it as upcoming, not to report it
as done.

Readers'-corner pattern (11 of 12 cores): the W43 corner printed a letter explicitly
labeled an **editor's example** (编辑部示例信 / editor's example), and promised that
the next issue prints **real readers' letters** plus the **best P8 writing**. The one
exception is French (see §FR), which was already past the demo stage in W43.
