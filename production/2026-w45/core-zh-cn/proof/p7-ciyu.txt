A2 · 双11与冬天 · Shopping
Shopping words for this week: 现货
(xiànhuò, in stock), 直降 (zhíjiàng, a
direct cut), 立减 (lìjiǎn, off at checkout),
结算页 (jiésuàn yè, the checkout page).
Winter words: 立冬 (lìdōng, Start of
Winter), 补冬 (bǔdōng, eating well before
winter), 厚衣服 (hòu yīfu, warm clothes).
Measure words still matter: 一件毛衣 (a
sweater), 一条围巾 (a scarf), 一部电影 (a film).

B1 · 时间与日期 · Time & dates
Chinese gives times big → small: year,
month, day, weekday, part of day, hour.
Examples from this issue:
「11月7日星期六17点52分」 (Sat Nov 7,
17:52), 「11月10日星期二20点」 (Tue Nov
10, 20:00), 「11月15日凌晨3点」 (Nov 15, 3
a.m.). The biggest unit first, nothing out
of order.
So English «Saturday evening, Nov 7»
becomes 11月7日星期六晚上.

B2 · 读新闻 · Reading the news
When a story involves a claim （说法）,
separate three kinds of sentence: claims
（说法）, data （数据） and
advertisements （广告）. Page 6 is the
example: 「最低价」 is a claim, the
practice baskets' 270元 is practice data,
and the checkout page shows what you
really pay.
Ask of every price: whose number is it, and
where is it written?

易错诊所 · Error clinic
「他高兴的跳起来」
→ 「他高兴地跳起来。」
地 (de) marks how an action is done.
「他跑的很快」
→ 「他跑得很快。」
得 (de) links verb + degree: 跑得快.
「我买了三只毛衣个」
→ 「我买了三件毛衣。」
Order: number + measure word + noun.
The final 个 is one too many; sweaters take
件.
「立冬在星期六十一月七日」
→ 「立冬在11月7日，星期六。」
Times go big → small: weekday last.
本周句式 · Sentence frame
「先看评价，再下单。」(First check
the reviews, then order.) The frame
先…再… gives the order of actions.
More: 「先看天气，再出门。」
Frame: 先 … 再 … — first X, then Y.
