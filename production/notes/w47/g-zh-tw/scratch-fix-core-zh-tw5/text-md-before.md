Notes｜Next Sunday (Nov 22) is Xiaoxue

(Minor Snow), the 20th solar term and the

second of winter: deeper winter, time to dress

warmly. After Xiaoxue the northeast monsoon

grows stronger and the weather gets colder

day by day. Keep your head, neck and feet

warm; a 10-15 minute warm foot soak in the

evening helps you sleep.
