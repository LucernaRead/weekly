On Monday, November 16, I wore odd socks to school — one red, one blue. My teacher
smiled and said, “Break the silence.” In the evening, we watched the jungle on television
together with my family. On Tuesday, we read about the election in class: the Democrats
won the House with 254 seats. On Friday, we will watch the new Hunger Games film. It is
a busy week of votes, socks and stories — and I am learning to say it all in English.
