< What does “called” mean? A call is a news organisation's judgement: the maths is clear, and the candidate behind cannot catch up. It is not the official result. The official result comes when each state certifies its count — between November 10 and December 18, with no fixed date in some states. > What does “called” mean? A call is a news organisation's judgement: the maths is clear, and the candidate behind cannot catch up. It is not the official result. The official result comes when each state certifies its count — between November 10 and December 18, with no fixed date in some states. In Georgia, a Senate race with no majority winner goes to a runoff on December 1 — but this time Jon Ossoff won reelection with about 59% of the vote, so there was no runoff. Mississippi has no runoff in a regular election: the candidate with the most votes wins.
