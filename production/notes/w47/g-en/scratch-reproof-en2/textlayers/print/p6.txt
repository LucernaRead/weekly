What does “called” mean? A call is a
news organisation's judgement: the
maths is clear, and the candidate
behind cannot catch up. It is not the
official result. The official result comes
when each state certifies its count —
between November 10 and December
18, with no fixed date in some states.
In Georgia, a Senate race with no
majority winner goes to a runoff on
December 1. Mississippi has no
runoff in a regular election: the
candidate with the most votes wins.

HOW LONG?

House: 2 years, all 435 seats each
midterm. Senate: 6 years, about a third
each election. President: 4 years.
Governor: usually 4 years.
