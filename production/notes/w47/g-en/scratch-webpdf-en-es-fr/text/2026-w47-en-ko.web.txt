Write a short text (80 to 100 words): “My Monday, November 16”. Tell us: did you wear
odd socks, what did you watch on television, what did you read about the election — and
one sentence about the film on Friday. The best texts will appear in VOL.5 (page 7,
“Readers' Corner”).

smiled and said, “Break the silence.” In the evening, we watched the jungle on television

won the House with 254 seats. On Friday, we will watch the new Hunger Games film. It is
a busy week of votes, socks and stories — and I am learning to say it all in English.
