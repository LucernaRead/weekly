## Adjudications (for the independent proofreader)

- **Badge is Latin-only:** the core badge's Chinese half (“简体中文正文 ·
  英语讲解”) is replaced wholesale by the Latin badge, per the W45
  precedent — otherwise the word “Nederlands” would add CJK characters
  the core does not have and fail strict-CJK. No Chinese reader-facing
  label for the explanation language is printed.
- **P4 C-head shortened:** “Werken met het programma” → “Lees het
  programma” (394.2 px at 25 pt).
- **P8 D-head shortened:** “Lezen en antwoorden” (442.6 px at 24 pt) →
  “Lees, antwoord” (415.6 px).
- **P2 calendar title shortened twice:** #3 “De data van deze week”
  (ends x = 1446.6, 7.1 px into the valley photo) → “Data van deze week”.
  #3 KIT claimed this “ends x = 1413.2, 26.3 px clear” — that 26.3 px was
  measured to the **photo** (x = 1439.5), not to the **box edge**
  (x = 1411.5); proof1 re-measured the title at ink x = 1413.25, still
  **1.75 px over the box edge**. fix1 (2026-11-11) shortened it again to
  “Data deze week” (ink ends x = 1366.9, **+44.5 px** clear of the box
  edge; render pixels +33.35 pt @100dpi). Box-edge measurement is the
  only valid clearance here — distance to the photo is not clearance.
- **P5 high-frequency-words title — #3 miss, fix1 repair:** #3 never
  mentioned the P5 box title “这一周的高频词　Woorden van de week”;
  proof1 measured its ink at x = 988.25, **27.25 px over the box edge**
  (961.0), the `k` of `week` fully on white paper. fix1 shortened the
  Dutch to “Woorden” (the Chinese 这一周 already carries “this week”;
  ink ends x = 843.0, **+118.0 px** clear; render pixels +90.03 pt
  @100dpi).
- **P7 teaser fixed in two places (worst of the week, 2641.82 → 2586.30):**
  the Dutch col-3 stack runs tallest of all editions. (a) The letters-box
  note dropped the redundant “in VOL.4” (“VOL.3 beloofde echte brieven
  in VOL.4; …” → “VOL.3 beloofde echte brieven; …”), 7 lines → 6,
  saving 29 px. (b) Error-clinic row 3 shortened (“Volgorde: getal +
  telwoord + zelfstandig naamwoord.” → “Volgorde: getal + telwoord +
  naamwoord.”), 2 lines → 1, saving 26.52 px. Root cause, verified by
  wrap counts: the four error-clinic notes all wrap to 2 lines in
  Dutch, so the col-3 error box (eh) is taller than in the other
  editions; the pattern/structure/letters boxes are the same height
  everywhere (stack 659.5).

  against a 2560 bottom; the seven notes cut to one line bring it to
  2491.1 — exactly the core's value. Q14/Q87 share one note string
  (applied once in each of `harvested.json` and the two edition files).
- **P4 gloss NOT shortened:** the Dutch gloss (“de laatste vier teams,
  de halvefinalisten”) is 6 lines under the “Notities｜” prefix and
  clears the “GEN，” line — no fix needed.
- **P2 word cards checked by measurement:** all 4 example sentences are
  one line at 19 pt in the 378.5 px card width — no fix needed.
- **“Sport” is Dutch, not leaked English:** the section header
