# WEEKLY-KIT — Lucerna Russian Weekly, VOL.4 (W47 en)

## 1. Spec

- **Edition:** Russian target-language core, VOL.4; reader language English (en).
- **Issue week:** Monday 16 November – Sunday 22 November 2026. **Issue date:** Monday 16 November 2026.
- **Pairing:** target language Russian × reader native language English (en).
  Masthead `Lucerna`; the top strip reads `КАЖДУЮ НЕДЕЛЮ ПО-РУССКИ · RUSSIAN TEXT · ENGLISH EXPLANATIONS`; the footer on every page reads `Lucerna · RUSSIAN WEEKLY VOL.4 · Russian text · English explanations` with `Page N of 8`.
- **Explanation-layer convention:** all explanation is in English — glosses, exercise instructions, answer notes, section furniture and footers. The Russian article text, exercise stems/keys and golden sentences are frozen from the W47 core; only the explanation layer was rewritten.
- **Format:** 8 pages (multiple of 4), 1950×2700 px per page, 4 columns, warm ivory paper, dark olive-green accents. Pipeline: Python → SVG → CairoSVG → PNG/PDF.
- **Exercises:** **150 total, continuously numbered 1–150**, registered in code and rendered as the P8 answer key. By page: P1 10, P2 18, P3 18, P4 18, P5 18, P6 18, P7 12, P8 38. By level: A2 50, B1 76, B2 24. By type: fill-in 54, multiple choice 42, matching 20, sentence building 12, rewrite 4, cloze 13, translation 4, writing 1.
  P8 model writing text (“Моя неделя 16–22 ноября”) = **97 words** (brief: 80–100).
- **P7 golden sentences:** 8 sentences, each occurring exactly twice in the issue — once in its own article, once in the P7 “ФРАЗЫ НЕДЕЛИ” list (verified programmatically on page-joined SVG text, §7). All 8 come from this issue's own articles: P1 ×2, P2 ×1, P3 ×1, P4 ×1, P5 ×1, P6 ×2. Page references on P7 are kept in Russian (`Страница N`) in every edition.
- **No invented facts.** No standings are printed beyond the round-14 table the core carries. Round-14 results are the issue's lead (played 6–8 Nov, before press time 10 Nov). The P6 chart is labelled practice data (ЧЕМ ЗАЙМУТСЯ НА ЭТОЙ НЕДЕЛЕ? + “This is a practice survey made up by the editors for the exercises — not real statistics.”) and sums to 100% (38/26/18/18) with proportional bars.
