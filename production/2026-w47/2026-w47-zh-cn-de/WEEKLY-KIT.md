## Adjudications (for the independent proofreader)

- **Badge is Latin-only:** the core badge's Chinese half (“简体中文正文 ·
  英语讲解”) is replaced wholesale by the Latin badge, per the W45 DE
  precedent — otherwise the word “德语” would add a CJK character the core
  does not have and fail strict-CJK. No Chinese reader-facing label for
  the explanation language is printed.
- **Press-time line shortened:** “Redaktionsschluss: 10. Nov. — Finale
  offen” (the full German line, 531 px, ran past the page edge).
- **P2 calendar title shortened:** “Kalender der Woche” (the longer
  “Diese Woche im Kalender”, 468.9 px, was covered 36 px by the
  neighbouring photo, hiding “der”).
- **P4 gloss shortened:** “die letzten vier Teams” (the “, die
  Halbfinalisten” tail made the Hinweise box 7 lines tall and covered the
  article text “GEN，” below it).
- **P2 “Practice example” corrected:** the table's one reversed entry
  (English left, Chinese translated) was fixed in all 9 languages to
  “<X>　练习例子”; the German title is now “Übungsbeispiel　练习例子”.
- **P7 teaser line pitch 120 → 112:** the German col-3 text runs ~49 px
  taller than the core; at pitch 120 the teaser band ended at 2615.3,
  crossing the footer rule (2596); at 112 it ends at 2583.3, clear.
- **P8 D-head font 24 → 22:** the “D 读一读” head measured 455.2 px
  against a 422.5 px column; at 22 it is 417.3 px.
- **“Sport” is German, not leaked English:** the section header
  “体育　Sport” is a covered table entry whose German rendering is
  identically “Sport”. Leak scan (all 275 translate sources against the
  final SVGs and .text.md) finds 0 English remnants; the only other hit
  is the documented-excluded P1 welcome line (pure Chinese body text).
- **“Level A2/B1/B2” kept:** the English level names are retained as in
  the shipped W46 editions.
