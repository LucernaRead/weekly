## 1. Spec

- **Edition:** Russian target-language core, VOL.4; reader language Japanese (ja).
- **Issue week:** Monday 16 November – Sunday 22 November 2026. **Issue date:** Monday 16 November 2026.
- **Pairing:** target language Russian × reader native language Japanese (ja).
  Masthead `Lucerna`; the top strip reads `КАЖДУЮ НЕДЕЛЮ ПО-РУССКИ · 毎週一号 · ロシア人が読んでいるロシア語を読む`; the footer on every page reads `Lucerna · ロシア語週刊 VOL.4 · 本文はロシア語 · 解説は日本語` with `{n}ページ（全8ページ）`.

- **P7 golden sentences:** 8 sentences, each occurring exactly twice in the issue — once in its own article, once in the P7 “ФРАЗЫ НЕДЕЛИ” list (verified programmatically on page-joined SVG text, §7).
