  core does not have and fail strict-CJK. No Chinese reader-facing label
  for the explanation language is printed.

  scegli” (377.1 px at 25 pt against a 422.5 px column; the infinitive

  (407.8 px at 24 pt).

  “Date della settimana” (ends x = 1424.3, 15.2 px clear of the valley
  photo at x = 1439.5; the longer title reached 61.9 px into the photo).

  the keys flow ended at 2604.2 against a 2560 bottom; six notes cut to
  one line (Q150 is the `gen_tables.py` f-string) bring it to 2506.4.

  one line at 19 pt in the 378.5 px card width — no fix needed, unlike
  the French card.
- **“Sport” is Italian, not leaked English:** the section header
  “体育　Sport” is a covered table entry whose Italian rendering is
  identically “Sport”. Leak scan (all 275 translate sources against the
  final SVGs and .text.md) finds 0 English remnants; the only other hit
  is the identical “悬疑 = suspense, b” rendering.

  the shipped W46 editions.
