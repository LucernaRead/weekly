## Masthead decision — why "Die"
The article follows the target language (SOP): French = Le Lucerna (French-only), English = The Lucerna. German: **Die Lucerna** — Lucerna is a Latin feminine noun, and its German cognate image is *die Laterne* (femininine), so the feminine article "Die" is used. Article-less target languages (RU/ZH/JA/KO) take no article.
