Lucerna VOL.1 introduces Russian through the first snow of the season: «Первый снег» sets the scene for an issue on autumn life in Russia and the long New Year holidays ahead — twelve days of новогодние каникулы to plan for. Written for English-speaking learners, the Russian texts are graded from A2 to B2, with vocabulary, grammar and cultural notes in English and 150 exercises across eight pages. A practical route into Russian reading practice through the season Russians are actually living, not a generic winter theme.
