In Le Lucerna VOL.1, English-speaking learners read French through the turn of the season: the passage à l’heure d’hiver on Sunday, when France recule d’une heure, the vacances de la Toussaint with their chrysanthèmes, and Halloween as it is actually marked in France. Further pages follow the présidentielle 2027 sondages, Victor Wembanyama, and fans awaiting GTA VI. Graded A2 to B2 with 145 exercises and English explanations throughout, the issue suits anyone who wants to learn French by reading the week’s real stories.
