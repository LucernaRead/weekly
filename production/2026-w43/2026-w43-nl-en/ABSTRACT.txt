De Lucerna VOL.1 packs a full Dutch week into one issue for English-speaking learners: herfstvakantie, Dutch Design Week in Eindhoven under the theme R.A.W., the 30th Amsterdam Dance Event, and the switch to wintertijd — the klok verzetten, with the rhyme “Voorjaar vooruit, najaar achteruit” to remember it by. Television and football close the week with Expeditie Robinson and PSV–Feyenoord. Dutch texts are graded A2 to B2 and backed by 150 exercises with explanations in English, making it structured Dutch reading practice built from the Netherlands’ own calendar.
