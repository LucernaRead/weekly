**Issue**: VOL.1 · **Week**: Monday 19 – Sunday 25 October 2026
**Pairing**: target language **Dutch** × reader native language **English** (matrix cell NL × EN).
**Masthead**: **De Lucerna** (blackletter) — the article follows the target language (De is the Dutch convention recorded for this paper).
