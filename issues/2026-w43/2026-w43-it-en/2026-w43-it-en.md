# VOL.1 · Italian text · English explanations

## Abstract

La Lucerna VOL.1 opens with an extra hour of sleep: Italy returns to l’ora solare, lancette indietro, and the issue uses that familiar ritual to practise Italian at A2 to B2 level. A second thread explains the Manovra 2027, the legge di bilancio then being debated, in language learners can follow. With 150 exercises, English explanations and vocabulary drawn from the week’s coverage, this is Italian reading practice for English speakers who want real topics — time, money and daily life — instead of phrasebook Italian.
