# VOL.1 · Korean text · English explanations

## Abstract

Lucerna VOL.1 catches Korea between seasons: Typhoon Champi approaching in the forecasts, autumn foliage at its 단풍 peak, and the 수능 countdown week for students nationwide. Aimed at English-speaking learners, the Korean texts run from A2 to B2, with 150 exercises and vocabulary and grammar explanations in English. The issue works as Korean reading practice drawn from what Korea was watching, photographing and preparing for that week — weather warnings, maple colour reports and exam-season routines — with every text checked against the level it claims.
