# VOL.1 · Portuguese text · English explanations

## Abstract

A Lucerna VOL.1 is a Brazilian Portuguese weekly for English-speaking learners, centred on decision Sunday: the segundo turno of the eleições on 25 October, explained step by step — como votar, the e-Título app and the urna eletrônica — after the final debate on Globo. Other pages look ahead to the ENEM in November, follow A Fazenda and the Copa Sul-Americana semi-final between Vasco and Boca, and note the guitar’s return in search trends. Graded A2 to B2, with 150 exercises and English support throughout — Portuguese reading practice tied to Brazil’s actual week.
