# Die Lucerna VOL.1 · German text · English explanations

Eine Stunde geschenkt — the clock change and the autumn holidays.

## Abstract

Die Lucerna VOL.1 is a German reading weekly for English-speaking learners, built around the week Germany turns its clocks back. On 25 October the Zeitumstellung moves the hour from 03:00 to 02:00, and the issue follows that extra hour of sleep through Herbstferien travel, autumn weather and everyday German life. Texts run from A2 to B2, supported by 150 graded exercises, German vocabulary notes and grammar explanations in English. A steady way to build German reading practice with real, current material rather than textbook dialogues.

German text with explanations written in English. 150 exercises, 8 pages, levels A2 to B2. Produced under the Lucerna Weekly SOP: trend-first sourcing in the target-language ecosystem, a full independent proofread to zero problems, and a geometric layout audit.
