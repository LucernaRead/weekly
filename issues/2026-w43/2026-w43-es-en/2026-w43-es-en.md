# La Lucerna VOL.1 · Spanish text · English explanations

One Sunday, three events — the clock change, El Clásico and F1 in Austin.

## Abstract

La Lucerna VOL.1 gathers one very full Sunday for learners of Spanish: the cambio de hora, when clocks go back and Spain settles into horario de invierno, El Clásico kicking off at 21:00, and Formula 1 in Austin. Each story is retold in clear, graded Spanish from A2 to B2, with English explanations, vocabulary support and 150 exercises across the issue. It is Spanish reading practice anchored in events learners will hear discussed all week, from the reloj to the racetrack.
